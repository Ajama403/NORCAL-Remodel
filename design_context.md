# Site Identity and Logo Strategy

## Logo Description
The website's logo features the text **"MODERN KITCHENS AND BATH"** in a bold, clean, black sans-serif geometric font.

To the left of the text is an orange line-art graphic featuring three conjoined peaked roofs (houses):
- The left section depicts a kitchen environment (range/oven with a hood).
- The middle section is a tall window/door.
- The right section depicts a bathroom environment (a bathtub with a showerhead).

## Design Suggestions for Claude
- **Color Palette:** Use the logo's high-contrast Black, White, and vibrant Orange (e.g., `#F2994A` or `#F9A826` depending on exact hex match) as the primary accent color.
- **Typography:** Use a bold, modern geometric sans-serif font for headers (like Montserrat, Poppins, or Inter) to match the logo's typography. 
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, modern, and structural, playing on the architectural lines of the logo's house motif.
